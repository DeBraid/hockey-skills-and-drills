    Privacy

    An account stores the name, email address, and profile photo from the sign-in provider, plus the practice plans you save. Share links do not include your notes. Saved plans are visible only to you. Delete account removes that profile and every plan saved to it.

    To see which drills get used, the site counts page views and plan actions with a random ID kept in this browser. It does not use cookies for this and does not record your email, IP address, or device details.
