Zone Exit: Reverse to Partner

Bounce it behind the net to your partner. D2 goes up the weak-side wall.

  • Half ice
  • Tactics
  • Breakout
  • Passing
  • Defence

Don't jam it up the strong-side wall. Go behind the net, then either skate the weak side or reverse it to your partner.

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Shoulder check before you get the puck.
  • The strong-side wall is where their pressure is. Don't jam it up there — their D is pinching.
  • Use the net as a shield. Bounce or reverse the puck behind it to your partner.
  • D2 talks, calls "over", and stays in the far corner.
  • The winger on the weak-side boards shows a target with the stick.

How it runs

  1. Setup: Same as Zone Exit: Skate the Weak Side. D1 has the puck in the strong-side corner. D2 stays in the far corner. The centre is low in support. One winger is on each wall. Their forward pressures D1, and their D pinches at the strong-side point.
  2. D1 gets the puck and shoulder checks. D2 stays on the far side and calls "over".
  3. D1 carries toward the net and bounces the puck off the end boards behind the net to D2 in the other corner.
  4. D2 passes up the weak-side wall to the winger, who shows a target and carries toward the blue line.
