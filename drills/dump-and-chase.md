# Dump and Chase

When the strong side is swarmed, dump to the weak-side corner and go get it.

## Coaching points

- Dump only when there is no lane. If you have ice, carry it in or pass.
- Dump to the weak-side corner — the one opposite you and the swarm. The defenders are stacked on the puck side, so the far corner is open.
- Chip it so the puck dies in the corner. A hard rim can go to the goalie or all the way around, and they will play it first.
- F2 and F3 both go hard to the puck in the weak-side corner. Win the race.
- The first one to the puck fires a hard pass to the back post.
- F1, stick on the ice at the back post.
- Attacking D: stay back near our blue line. Don't pinch up during the retrieval.

## How it runs

1. Setup: 5-on-5. F1 has the puck on the strong side and leads the rush by a few strides. F2 and F3 trail slightly, spread evenly across the ice in the far-wall and middle lanes. The attacking D stay back near their own blue line. A goalie is in the far net.
2. F1 carries up the strong-side wall toward the offensive blue line. F2 and F3 trail in their lanes.
3. Two D and a backchecker swarm F1 and take away the lane.
4. F1 dumps the puck into the weak-side corner, the one opposite the swarm.
5. F2 and F3 both go hard to the corner to retrieve the puck. F1 skates to the back post. The attacking D stay back near their own blue line.
6. F2, first to the puck, fires a hard pass from the corner to F1 at the back post. F1 taps it in.
