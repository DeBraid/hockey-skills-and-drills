# Dump and Chase

When the strong side is swarmed, dump to the weak-side corner and go get it.

## Coaching points

- Dump only when there is no lane. If you have ice, carry it in or pass.
- Dump to the weak-side corner — the one opposite you and the swarm. The defenders are stacked on the puck side, so the far corner is open.
- Chip it so the puck dies in the corner. A hard rim can go to the goalie or all the way around, and they will play it first.
- First forward to the puck pressures and wins the race. The second forward supports — don't both go to the same corner.
- Attacking D: hold the blue line. Don't chase the dump all the way down.

## How it runs

1. Setup: 5-on-5. F1 has the puck on the strong side just past centre. F2 is on the weak side, F3 in the middle, and the attacking D trail. A goalie is in the far net.
2. F1 carries up the strong-side wall toward the offensive blue line. F2 and F3 skate up in their lanes.
3. Two D and a backchecker swarm F1 and take away the lane.
4. F1 dumps the puck into the weak-side corner, the one opposite the swarm.
5. F2 hunts the puck down and retrieves it. F3 supports. The attacking D hold the blue line.
