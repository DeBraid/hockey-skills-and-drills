# 3-on-2 Rush

Three forwards attack two D. Puck wide, middle drives the net, far wing goes back post.

## Coaching points

- Forwards: spread out across the ice. Three lanes.
- Middle: drive the net hard after you pass.
- Far wing: go to the back post for the rebound.
- D: one takes the puck carrier, one guards the front of the net.
- Shoot to make a rebound.

## How it runs

1. Three forward lines at centre ice, two D at the blue line.
2. Go! The D skate backwards.
3. The middle passes the puck to a wing.
4. The middle drives the net, the far wing goes back post.
5. The wing shoots. Forwards switch lines.
