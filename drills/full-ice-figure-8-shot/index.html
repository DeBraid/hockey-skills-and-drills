Full-Ice Figure-8 Shooting

Up the wall, swing through centre, curl around the far circle, cut to the net and shoot. Both corners go.

  • Full ice
  • Skating
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Keep your feet moving the whole way. Crossovers on the curves.
  • Head up crossing centre ice: the other line is coming the other way.
  • Curl tight around the outside of the circle with the puck on your forehand.
  • Cut to the net with speed and shoot to score. Follow it for a rebound.
  • Start without pucks if the group is new to it.

How it runs

  1. Setup: a line with pucks in two opposite corners and a goalie in each net.
  2. Carry the puck up the wall to the blue line.
  3. Swing up through centre ice and back down to the far blue line.
  4. Curl around the outside of the far faceoff circle.
  5. Cut to the net and shoot.
  6. Join the line in the corner at that end. Both corners go at the same time; the next player goes when the one ahead reaches centre.
