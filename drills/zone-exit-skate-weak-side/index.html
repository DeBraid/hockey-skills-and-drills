Zone Exit: Skate the Weak Side

Don't rim the strong-side wall. Go behind the net and hit the weak-side winger.

  • Half ice
  • Tactics
  • Breakout
  • Passing
  • Defence

Don't jam it up the strong-side wall. Go behind the net, then either skate the weak side or reverse it to your partner.

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Shoulder check before you get the puck.
  • The strong-side wall is where their pressure is. Don't jam it up there — their D is pinching, and a rim almost always turns over.
  • Use the net as a shield. Go behind it and skate out the weak side.
  • D2 talks, calls "over", and stays in the far corner.
  • The winger on the weak-side boards shows a target with the stick.

How it runs

  1. Setup: Our defensive zone, half ice. D1 has the puck in the strong-side corner. D2 is in the far corner. The centre is low in support. One winger is on each wall. Their forward is coming to pressure D1, and their D is pinching at the strong-side point. A goalie is in our net.
  2. D1 gets the puck and shoulder checks. Their forward closes, and their D pinches on the strong-side wall.
  3. Don't rim it up the strong side. That pass goes to their pinching D.
  4. Instead, D1 goes behind the net, using it as a shield, and skates out the weak side.
  5. D1 passes to the winger on the weak-side boards, who shows a target and carries toward the blue line.
