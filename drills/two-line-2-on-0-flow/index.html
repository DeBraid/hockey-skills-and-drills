Two-Line 2-on-0 Flow

Partners pass back and forth all the way up the ice, then attack 2-on-0 and shoot. Both ends go.

  • Full ice
  • Passing
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Pass hard and flat, a stick-length ahead of your partner.
  • Give a target: stick on the ice, blade facing the passer.
  • Stay in your lane and keep your speed. Don't drift toward your partner.
  • Finish: the shooter shoots to score, the other player drives the net for a rebound.

How it runs

  1. Setup: at each end, a centre line and a winger line wait below the goal line on the same side, with pucks at the centre line. A goalie in each net.
  2. The centre passes to the winger and both skate up the ice in their lanes.
  3. The winger passes back to the centre.
  4. The centre passes to the winger again across the far blue line.
  5. The winger passes to the centre in the middle. The centre shoots and the winger drives the net.
  6. Both join the lines at that end. The pair from the other end goes at the same time on the other half of the ice.
