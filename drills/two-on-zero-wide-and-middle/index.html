2-on-0 Wide and Middle

One goes wide with the puck, one drives the middle to the back post. Shoot (or pass), and both go to the net.

  • Half ice
  • Zone entry
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Carrier: wide with speed, then cut in with your head up.
  • Middle player: drive hard to the back post, stick on the ice.
  • Shoot for a rebound, or pass across if the back post is open.
  • Both players go to the net after the shot.
  • Switch lanes every rep so everyone plays both roles.

How it runs

  1. A wide line at the red line by the boards with pucks, and a middle line in the centre lane. A goalie in net.
  2. The wide player carries over the blue line along the boards while the partner drives the middle lane.
  3. The carrier cuts in; the middle player keeps going to the back post.
  4. The carrier shoots (or passes across to the back post). Both go to the net for the rebound.
  5. Switch lines: the carrier joins the middle line and the middle player joins the wide line.
