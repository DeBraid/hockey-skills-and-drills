Neutral-Zone Regroup: D to D to Swing

D passes across to D, the forward swings through the middle, takes the pass and attacks.

  • Half ice
  • Passing
  • Breakout
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • D: hard, flat pass across. Look up before you pass.
  • F: time your swing so you're moving up ice when the pass comes.
  • F: show your stick as a target.
  • Attack with speed and shoot.
  • Switch which D starts with the puck.

How it runs

  1. Two D lines near centre ice with pucks, a forward line on the wall at the blue line.
  2. D passes across to the other D.
  3. The forward swings back through the middle.
  4. The D hits the forward with a pass.
  5. The forward attacks and shoots.
