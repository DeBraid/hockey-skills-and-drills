# Zone Exit: Skate the Weak Side

Coach dumps it in. D1 wheels behind the net and hits the weak-side winger.

**Tags:** `half-ice`, `tactics`, `breakout`, `passing`, `defence`

Don't jam it up the strong-side wall. Go behind the net, then either skate the weak side or reverse it to your partner.

## Coaching points

- The coach dumps the puck into the strong-side corner. D1 goes to get it.
- Shoulder check before you get the puck.
- The pylons are their pressure. Don't rim it up that wall.
- Use the net as a shield. Go behind it and skate out the weak side.
- D2 talks, calls "over", and stays in the far corner.
- The winger on the weak-side boards shows a target with the stick.

## How it runs

1. Setup: Our defensive zone, half ice. D1 starts in front of the net, D2 in the far corner, and the centre low in support. Winger lines stand on each wall, and a waiting D line stands at the blue line. The coach has pucks at the blue. Pylons mark their pressure on the strong side. A goalie is in our net.
2. The coach dumps the puck into the strong-side corner. D1 skates to it and shoulder checks.
3. D1 wheels behind the net, using it as a shield, and skates out the weak side.
4. D1 passes to the winger on the weak-side boards, who shows a target and carries toward the blue line.
5. The next D is ready.
