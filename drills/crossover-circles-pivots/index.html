Crossover Circles: Pivot Race

Race: one lap around the circle, pivot at every cone. Long pass from your line, shoot. First goal wins.

  • Skating
  • Passing
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • One lap, pivot at every cone.
  • Pivot: open your hips toward the middle of the circle, keep your feet moving, eyes up.
  • Backwards: stay low with your shoulders square, push with crossunders.
  • Turn forwards after the lap and show your stick for the pass.
  • Catch the long pass cleanly, then shoot to score. First goal wins.

How it runs

  1. Same setup as the Crossover Circles Race: a line on each neutral-zone faceoff dot near the blue line with pucks. Put 3 cones on each circle, spread evenly.
  2. Race! Skate into the zone and go forwards to cone 1. Pivot to backwards.
  3. Backwards to cone 2. Pivot to forwards.
  4. Forwards to cone 3. Pivot to backwards.
  5. Backwards to finish the lap, turn forwards and take a long pass from the next player on the blue-line dot.
  6. Shoot! First goal wins. The passer goes next and the shooter goes to the back of the line.
