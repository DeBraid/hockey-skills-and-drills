# Crossover Circles Race

Race around your circle with crossovers, then race to the one puck. Winner attacks, loser plays D. 1-on-1, shoot.

## Coaching points

- Knees bent and chest up. Push with the outside leg and cross the inside leg over.
- Lean into the circle and keep your stick on the ice.
- Exit the circle at full speed and race to the puck.
- Win the race? Protect the puck and attack the net.
- Lose the race? You're the D. Get between the attacker and the net.
- Switch directions every round so both crossovers get work.

## How it runs

1. Split into two lines, one standing on each neutral-zone faceoff dot near the blue line. The coach puts ONE puck in the middle of the ice, halfway between the blue line and the tops of the circles.
2. Race! The front skater on each line skates into the zone and all the way around their faceoff circle with crossovers.
3. Exit the circle and race to the puck.
4. The first one there picks it up and attacks. The other racer plays D.
5. 1-on-1 to the net. Shoot!
6. Both racers go to the back of their line. The coach puts a new puck down. Next round, go the other way.
7. Variation (step 2): see Crossover Circles: Pivot Race. One lap around the circle with a pivot at every cone, then race to one loose puck.
