Goalie Warm-up Shooting Sequence

Shooters take turns in a set order: slot, left angle, right angle, then in tight. Shoot to warm up the goalie, not to beat him.

  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • One shot at a time. Wait until the goalie is set.
  • Low and on net: help the goalie warm up.
  • Hit the spot you're told: low blocker, low glove, pads.
  • Goalie: follow the puck all the way in.
  • Rotate spots so everyone shoots from all three.

How it runs

  1. Three shooting spots with pucks: the slot, one angle, the other angle. A waiting line at the blue line.
  2. Shot 1 from the slot, low.
  3. Shot 2 from one angle, low to the far side.
  4. Shot 3 from the other angle.
  5. Shot 4: carry in tight from the slot and shoot, then the rebound.
  6. Shooters rotate to the next spot.
