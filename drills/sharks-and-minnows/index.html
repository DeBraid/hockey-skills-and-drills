Sharks and Minnows

Minnows with pucks try to cross the zone. Sharks knock pucks away. Lose your puck and you become a shark.

  • Small-area game
  • Stickhandling

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Head up: see the sharks before they reach you.
  • Protect the puck: body between the shark and the puck.
  • Change speed and direction to get past.
  • Sharks: stick on the ice, take away space.
  • Keep it fair: stick on puck, no slashing.

How it runs

  1. Minnows with pucks line up on the blue line. Start with 2 sharks in the middle.
  2. Go! Minnows try to cross to the goal line.
  3. Sharks knock pucks away. If you lose your puck, you become a shark.
  4. Cross back. Keep going until one minnow is left.
