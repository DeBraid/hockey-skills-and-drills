# Breakout to 1-on-1

D skates it out and hits the F on the wall, then pivots and skates backward. 1-on-1 the length of the ice.

## Coaching points

- F: back to the boards on the wall, stick on the ice, catch it and go.
- D: hard, flat pass to the forehand, then a quick pivot to backward.
- D: keep your gap. Stay between the F and the net, stick on the ice.
- F: use your speed, attack the D's outside, and shoot to score.
- Both: go back to your lines after the shot, then switch D and F.

## How it runs

1. Setup: a D line with pucks in one corner and a forward line in the other corner of the same end. A goalie in each net.
2. The D skates the puck out of the corner while the F skates to the half-wall.
3. The D passes to the F on the wall, then pivots to skate backward.
4. The F carries up the wall while the D backs up through the middle.
5. 1-on-1 through the neutral zone: the D keeps a good gap.
6. The F tries to beat the D and shoots on the far goalie. Both go back to their lines.
