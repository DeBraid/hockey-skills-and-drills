# Circle Bump Passes

One lap around the circle with 4 forehand bump passes to the middle, then reverse for 4 on the backhand.

## Coaching points

- Knees bent the whole time. Stay low through every touch.
- Crossovers all the way around the lap. Don't glide into the pylon.
- Soft hands: give with the puck as it hits your stick.
- Head up: look at the passer before the puck comes.
- Quick bump: one touch back to the middle, on the forehand and on the backhand.
- Passer: lead the skater a little so the puck meets them at the pylon.
- Lap 1 is the forehand lap. For a left shot that's counter-clockwise; right shots can start clockwise so lap 1 is still the forehand.
- Coach note: the diagram draws lap 2 just inside the pylons so both laps show. On the ice, both laps go around the pylons.

## How it runs

1. Setup: use both end-zone circles. A passer stands on each faceoff dot with a few pucks. Put 4 pylons on each circle, at the top, bottom, left and right. A line of skaters waits beside each circle, toward the blue line.
2. Forehand lap: the skater (no puck) does one lap around the circle. At each pylon, take a pass from the middle and bump it straight back. That's 4 touches on the forehand.
3. Reverse: turn around and do a lap the other way. At each pylon, receive and bump back on the backhand. 4 more touches.
4. Go to the back of the line. The next skater goes. Rotate the passers every few turns.
