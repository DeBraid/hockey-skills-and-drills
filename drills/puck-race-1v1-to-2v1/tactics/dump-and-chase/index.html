Dump and Chase

Coach dumps to the far corner. F2 and F3 retrieve, F1 taps it in at the back post.

Coaching points

  • Lines in the neutral zone: F1 on the strong-side wall, F3 in the middle, F2 on the far wall.
  • The coach (or F1) dumps the puck into the far, weak-side corner.
  • The pylons are the swarm. Don't dump that side.
  • Chip it so the puck dies in the corner.
  • F2 and F3 both go hard to the puck. Win the race.
  • The first one to the puck fires a hard pass to the back post.
  • F1, stick on the ice at the back post.
  • Dump only when there is no lane. If you have ice, carry it in or pass.
  • Wrong way: a dump into the swarm, or trying to deke through three defenders, turns over.
  • Right way: dump to the weak-side corner — the one opposite you and the swarm.
  • Chip it so the puck dies in the corner. A hard rim can go to the goalie or all the way around.
  • F2 and F3 both go hard to the puck in the weak-side corner. Win the race.
  • The first one to the puck fires a hard pass to the back post.
  • F1, stick on the ice at the back post.
  • Attacking D: stay back near our blue line. Don't pinch up during the retrieval.

How it runs

  1. Setup: Three lines in the neutral zone — F1 on the strong-side wall, F3 in the middle, F2 on the far wall. A coach has pucks near centre ice. Three pylons mark a swarm on the strong side of the offensive blue line, with a passive D there. A goalie is in the far net.
  2. The coach dumps the puck into the far, weak-side corner.
  3. F2 and F3 race to the corner to retrieve it. F1 drives to the back post.
  4. F2, first to the puck, fires a hard pass to F1, who taps it in.
  5. The next three go.
  1. Setup: 5-on-5. F1 has the puck on the strong side and leads the rush by a few strides. F2 and F3 trail slightly, spread evenly across the ice. The attacking D stay back near their own blue line. A goalie is in each net.
  2. F1 carries up the strong-side wall toward the offensive blue line. F2 and F3 trail in their lanes.
  3. Two D and a backchecker swarm F1 and take away the lane.
  4. Wrong way: F1 dumps the puck into the swarm on the strong side. Their D takes it.
  5. Right way: From the same spot, F1 dumps the puck into the weak-side corner, the one opposite the swarm.
  6. F2 and F3 both go hard to the corner to retrieve the puck. F1 skates to the back post. The attacking D stay back near their own blue line.
  7. F2, first to the puck, fires a hard pass from the corner to F1 at the back post. F1 taps it in.
