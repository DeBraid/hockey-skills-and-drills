Olympic Breakout to 2-on-1

3-on-0 breakout to the blue line, tight turn around the cone, then back in 2-on-1 against the same D.

  • Half ice
  • Breakout
  • Passing
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • D: shoulder-check on the way to the puck, then a hard, flat pass to the winger's forehand.
  • Winger: back to the boards at the hash marks, stick on the ice.
  • Centre: swing low and curl up the middle with speed, ready for the pass.
  • Centre: go all the way around the cone, tight to it, puck on the inside, head up coming out.
  • D: gap up, then pivot and skate backward. Stay in the middle and take away the pass.
  • Forwards: one pass to beat the D, then shoot.

How it runs

  1. Setup: one D in front of the net and a cone on the blue line. Waiting lines stand in the neutral zone outside the blue line: D high, centres in the middle, wingers on the bottom boards. The coach has the pucks. Only the D, one winger and one centre go each time.
  2. The coach rims the puck behind the net. The D goes to get it and the winger skates down to the half-wall at the hash marks.
  3. The D passes to the winger on the wall while the centre swings low through the slot.
  4. The winger passes to the centre curling up the middle. The D gaps up toward the top of the circles.
  5. The centre carries the puck and the winger skates up to the blue line.
  6. Tight turn at the blue line: the centre loops around the cone with the puck and the winger turns on the wall side. The D pivots and skates backward.
  7. 2-on-1: the centre passes to the winger to beat the D, and the winger shoots.
  8. Everyone goes back to their line in the neutral zone and the next D steps in front of the net.
