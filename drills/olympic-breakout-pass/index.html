Olympic Breakout to 2-on-1

D gets the rim and hits the winger. Winger to the curling centre, out of the zone, then 2-on-1 against the D.

  • Breakout
  • Passing
  • Shooting
  • Zone entry

Diagram

Coaching points

  • D: shoulder-check on the way to the puck, then a hard, flat pass to the winger's forehand.
  • Winger: back to the boards at the hash marks, stick on the ice.
  • Centre: swing low and curl up the middle with speed, ready for the pass.
  • D: race up ice, pivot at the far blue line, and stay between the two forwards.
  • Forwards: one pass to beat the D, then shoot. Centre drives the net for a rebound.

How it runs

  1. Setup: one D in front of the net, the D line high in the zone, the winger line on the bottom boards near the blue line, and the centre line in the high middle. The coach has the pucks. Only the D, one winger and one centre go each time.
  2. The coach rims the puck behind the net. The D goes to get it and the winger skates down to the half-wall at the hash marks.
  3. The D passes to the winger on the wall while the centre swings low through the slot.
  4. The winger passes to the centre curling up the middle. The D skates hard up the far side of the ice.
  5. The centre carries the puck out of the zone up the middle and the winger skates up the wall. The D gets to the far blue line and pivots.
  6. 2-on-1: the forwards attack while the D skates backward.
  7. The centre passes to the winger, who shoots on the far goalie. The centre drives the net.
  8. Everyone goes back to their line and the next D steps in front of the net.
