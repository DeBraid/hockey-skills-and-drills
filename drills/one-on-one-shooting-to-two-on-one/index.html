Corner Pass to Half-Ice 1-on-1

Lines in both corners and at the centre cone. Centre passes to a corner, then defends. 1-on-1 in your half of the zone.

  • Half ice
  • Passing
  • Shooting
  • Defence

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Passer: a hard, flat pass to the corner player's stick, then go defend right away.
  • Attacker: catch it, get your feet moving up the wall, then attack the net with speed.
  • Stay in your half of the zone. The middle of the ice is the boundary.
  • D: stay between the attacker and the net, stick on the ice, take away the middle.
  • Shoot to score, then follow it for the rebound.

How it runs

  1. Three lines: one against the boards in each corner, and one in the middle of the ice at a cone level with the tops of the faceoff circles. Pucks with the centre line. A goalie in the net.
  2. The front centre player passes to the front of one corner line.
  3. The corner player carries up the wall. The passer steps across into that half to defend.
  4. 1-on-1: the attacker curls in and attacks the net, staying in their half of the zone. The D skates backwards and stays on the inside.
  5. The attacker shoots.
  6. Rotation: the attacker goes to the centre line, the D goes to the corner line they defended. The next centre player passes to the other corner.
