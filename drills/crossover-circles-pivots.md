# Crossover Circles: Pivot Race

Race: 1 circle clockwise, 1 circle the other way, then pivot at every cone (F, B, F, B, F). Pass, shoot.

## Coaching points

- Circle 1 clockwise, circle 2 counter-clockwise: crossovers both ways.
- Pivot at every cone you pass: forwards, backwards, forwards, backwards, forwards.
- Pivot: open your hips toward the middle of the circle, keep your feet moving, eyes up.
- Backwards: stay low with your shoulders square, push with crossunders.
- Catch the long pass from the blue-line dot, then shoot to score. First goal wins.

## How it runs

1. Same setup as the Crossover Circles Race: a line on each neutral-zone faceoff dot near the blue line with pucks. Put 3 cones on each circle, spread evenly.
2. Race! Skate into the zone and do one full circle clockwise with crossovers.
3. Then one full circle the other way (counter-clockwise).
4. Pivot lap, going clockwise. Forwards to cone 1 and pivot to backwards.
5. Backwards to cone 2, pivot to forwards. Forwards to cone 3, pivot to backwards.
6. Backwards to cone 1 again, pivot to forwards.
7. Skate forwards and take a long pass from the next player on the blue-line dot. Curl in and shoot. First goal wins. The passer goes next.
