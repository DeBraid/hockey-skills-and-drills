Triangle Passing on the Move

Groups of 3 skate up the ice in a triangle: middle to wing, across to the far wing, back to the middle, shoot.

  • Half ice
  • Passing
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Keep the triangle: wings wide, middle trailing a little.
  • Pass ahead of your teammate's stick so they don't slow down.
  • Stick on the ice, ready for the pass.
  • Middle player: drive the net hard after your pass.
  • Call for the puck.

How it runs

  1. Three lines at centre ice: middle (with pucks), left wing, right wing.
  2. All three go. The middle passes to a wing.
  3. That wing passes across to the far wing.
  4. The far wing passes to the middle player driving the net.
  5. The middle player shoots.
  6. Switch lines and skate back to centre.
