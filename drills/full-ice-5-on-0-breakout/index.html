Full-Ice 5-on-0 Breakout

Coach dumps in, five break out 5-on-0 and rush to the far net. On the whistle, the 3 F come back the other way with the other D pair.

  • Full ice
  • Breakout
  • Passing
  • Defence
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • D: shoulder check on the way back for the puck.
  • D-to-D behind the net when the forechecker would be on the puck side.
  • Winger: back to the boards, stop it, then bump it off the boards to the centre.
  • Centre: swing low through the middle with speed, stick on the ice.
  • Fill three lanes through the neutral zone and drive the net for rebounds.
  • On the whistle, forwards turn and go right away. D swap ends.

How it runs

  1. Setup: three forwards and a D pair in one end, the other D pair at the far end. A goalie in each net.
  2. The coach dumps the puck into the corner and a D retrieves.
  3. D-to-D behind the net.
  4. The D passes up the wall to the winger while the centre swings low.
  5. The winger bumps it off the boards to the centre.
  6. All five skate through the neutral zone and attack the far end 5-on-0 for a shot on net.
  7. On the whistle the three forwards come back the other way with the far-end D pair. The first pair stays at that end.
