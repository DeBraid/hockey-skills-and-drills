Zone Entries: Delay

Carry in wide, delay on the wall, then attack (top) or hit a catcher streaking to the slot (bottom).

  • Zone entry
  • Half ice
  • Passing
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Carry in wide with speed to push the D back.
  • Delay: stop and curl hard with the puck on your forehand, protecting it from the D.
  • Head up during the curl: look for the attack lane or the late player.
  • Catcher: time your streak so you hit the slot with speed, stick on the ice.
  • Pass to the stick in the slot; catch and shoot quickly.

How it runs

  1. Lines of forwards with pucks at the boards in the neutral zone. For the low-to-slot variation, a catcher line waits in the middle.
  2. Delay (top): carry in wide over the blue line.
  3. Stop and curl back up the wall to delay.
  4. Attack: cut to the slot and shoot.
  5. Delay, low-to-slot (bottom): carry in wide and delay the same way. The catcher streaks to the net; the carrier passes to the slot and the catcher shoots.
  6. The other two variations in this progression (delay, low-to-high to the slot; delay, low-to-high to the D) match Low-to-High: Give-and-Go and Low-to-High: D-to-D.
