# Olympic Breakout Pass

Coach rims it in. D gets it and passes to the winger on the wall, who hits the centre curling low. Carry it out.

## Coaching points

- D: shoulder-check on the way to the puck so you know where the winger is before you get there.
- D: hard, flat pass to the winger's forehand.
- Winger: back to the boards at the hash marks, stick on the ice, give a target.
- Centre: swing low through the middle and curl with speed, stick on the ice ready for the pass.
- The drill isn't done until the puck crosses the blue line.

## How it runs

1. Setup: one D in front of the net, the D line high in the zone, the winger line along the bottom boards near the blue line, and the centre line in the high middle. The coach has the pucks at the top of the zone.
2. The coach rims the puck around the boards behind the net. The D turns and goes to get it, and the winger skates down the wall to the half-wall at the hash marks.
3. The D shoulder-checks, picks up the puck beside the net and passes hard to the winger on the wall. At the same time the centre swings low through the slot.
4. The winger passes to the centre, who is curling up the middle.
5. The centre carries the puck out over the blue line while the winger skates up the wall.
6. Everyone goes back to their line and the next D steps in front of the net. Progression: carry it all the way and shoot on the far net, or add a pass back to the winger in the neutral zone.
