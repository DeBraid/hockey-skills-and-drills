Three-Lane Wave Shooting

Three lanes go one after another: carry in, shoot, then clear out to the side so the next shooter has room.

  • Half ice
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Head up while you carry; pick your spot before you shoot.
  • Shoot in stride, quick release.
  • Go when the player before you reaches the blue line.
  • After the shot, clear out to the side. Don't skate through the next shooter's lane.
  • Shoot to score: low blocker or low glove.

How it runs

  1. Three lines at the red line (top, middle and bottom lanes), each with pucks. A goalie in net.
  2. Lane 1 carries in and shoots.
  3. Lane 2 goes when lane 1 reaches the blue line, and shoots. Lane 1 clears out to the side.
  4. Lane 3 goes next and shoots. Lane 2 clears out.
  5. Everyone returns to their own lane's line. The next wave goes when the goalie is set.
