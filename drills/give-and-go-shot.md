# Give-and-Go to Shot

Skate up the wall, give-and-go with the coach, round the pylon, take the return pass and drive in for a shot.

## Coaching points

- Give a target: stick on the ice, blade facing the coach.
- Make eye contact with the passer before the puck comes.
- One-touch pass back: cushion it and give it right back.
- Keep your feet moving through the pylon; don't glide.
- Drive the net after the catch and shoot to score.

## How it runs

1. One line in the corner. A coach with pucks stands in the middle of the zone. A pylon near the red line on the same side.
2. The front player skates up the wall. The coach passes to them.
3. Give it right back to the coach (give-and-go) and keep skating toward the pylon.
4. Round the pylon. The coach passes again as you come out of the turn.
5. Drive in and shoot, then join the back of the line. Alternate sides each round.
