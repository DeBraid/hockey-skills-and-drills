Wide Entry, Drive the Net

Carry in wide over the blue line, keep your feet moving around the cone, cut to the net and shoot.

  • Half ice
  • Zone entry
  • Shooting

Watch

Open full screen

Diagram

Coaching points

  • Go wide with speed to push the defender back.
  • Keep your feet moving around the cone. No gliding.
  • Protect the puck on the turn: body between the cone and the puck.
  • Cut hard to the net and shoot in stride.
  • Follow your shot for the rebound.

How it runs

  1. One line at the red line by the boards, with pucks. A cone inside the blue line, wide near the bottom of the circle. A goalie in net.
  2. Carry the puck wide over the blue line with speed.
  3. Go around the cone with your feet moving, then cut to the net.
  4. Shoot, then drive the net for a rebound. Join the back of the line.
  5. Alternate sides each round. Progression: add a D at the blue line for a 1-on-1.
