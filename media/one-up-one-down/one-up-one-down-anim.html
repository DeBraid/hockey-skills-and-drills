1 Up / 1 Down

Gap and angling 1v1. D touches the goal line, F touches the blue line, then play it out.

Setup: F and D lines in the corner, coach with pucks

Step 1: on 'Go', D touches the goal line, F the blue line

Step 2: coach puts the puck in, F attacks, D gaps up

Step 3: F shoots (1 shot + 1 rebound), D angles

Flip: F joins the D line, D joins the F line

If D steals it: clear it or skate it past the top of the circles
