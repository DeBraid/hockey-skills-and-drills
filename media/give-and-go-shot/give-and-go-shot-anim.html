Give-and-Go to Shot

Skate up the wall, give-and-go with the coach, round the pylon, take the return pass and drive in for a shot.

Setup: line in the corner, coach with pucks

Step 1: skate up the wall, coach passes

Step 2: give it right back, keep going

Step 3: round the pylon, coach passes

Step 4: drive in and shoot

Back of the line, next player

Quick one-touch pass back, then go!
