Stationary Partner Passing

Partners 10-13 ft apart pass back and forth: forehand, then backhand, then switch sides. Step back as passes get crisp.

Setup: partners face each other, one puck

Step 1: forehand pass to your partner

Step 2: cushion it, pass it back

Step 3: now backhand, then switch sides

Crisp passes? Take a step back each.
