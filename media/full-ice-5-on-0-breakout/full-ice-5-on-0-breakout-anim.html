Full-Ice 5-on-0 Breakout

Coach dumps in, 5-on-0 breakout and rush to the far net. On the whistle, back the other way with the other D pair.

Setup: 3 F + D pair in the start end, other D pair at the far end

Step 1: Coach dumps it in, D retrieves

Step 2: D-to-D behind the net

Step 3: Up the wall to the winger, C swings

Step 4: Winger bumps it off the boards to the C

Step 5: All five through the neutral zone

Step 6: Attack 5-on-0 and shoot on the far net

Step 7: Whistle! Back the other way, D swap
