Special One II

Both ends start together: a forward + D give-and-go at one end, a 3-pass connection at the other, then a 1-on-1 one way and a 2-on-1 the other.

Setup: 5 lines, pucks at centre and in two corners

Step 1: Go together! Lead pass up the middle

Step 2: Around centre, pass to D, D wheels the net

Step 3: Around the circle, to D, D wheels, pass inside

Step 4: Timing! Return pass left, relay high right

Step 5: 1-on-1 to the left net, 2-on-1 to the right

Step 6: Shoot! Both D defend the rushes
