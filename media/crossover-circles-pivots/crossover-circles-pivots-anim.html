Crossover Circles: Pivot Race

Race: 1 circle clockwise, 1 circle the other way, then pivot at every cone (F, B, F, B, F). Pass, shoot.

Setup: lines on the blue-line dots, 3 cones per circle

Step 1: Race! 1 circle clockwise

Step 2: 1 circle the other way

Step 3: Forwards to cone 1, pivot

Step 4: Backwards to cone 2, pivot

Step 5: Forwards to cone 3, pivot

Step 6: Backwards to cone 1, pivot

Step 7: Forwards, long pass from your line

Step 8: Shoot! First goal wins

Passer goes next. Shooter to the back

Race! Next pair goes.
