Crossover Circles: Pivot Race

Race: one lap around the circle, pivot at every cone. Long pass from your line, shoot. First goal wins.

Setup: lines on the blue-line dots, 3 cones per circle

Step 1: Race! Forwards to cone 1, pivot

Step 2: Backwards to cone 2, pivot

Step 3: Forwards to cone 3, pivot

Step 4: Backwards, turn, take the pass

Step 5: Shoot! First goal wins

Passer goes next. Shooter to the back

Race! Next pair goes.
