Crossover Circles: Pivot Race

Race: one lap around the circle, pivot at every cone. Then race to the puck. First one there shoots.

Setup: lines on the blue-line dots, 1 puck

Step 1: Race! Forwards to cone 1, pivot

Step 2: Backwards to cone 2, pivot

Step 3: Forwards to cone 3, pivot

Step 4: Backwards, turn, race to the puck

Step 5: First to the puck shoots!
