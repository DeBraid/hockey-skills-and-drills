Goalie Warm-up Shooting Sequence

Shooters take turns in a set order: slot, left angle, right angle, then in tight. Shoot to warm up the goalie, not to beat him.

Setup: 3 shooting spots with pucks, goalie in the net

Shot 1: from the slot, low

Shot 2: from the angle, low far side

Shot 3: from the other angle

Shot 4: in tight, then the rebound

Shooters rotate to the next spot

Next shooter in. Wait until the goalie is set!
