Breakout: D to the Wall

F passes to the D, loops low and opens up on the wall. D skates it behind the net and hits the F, who attacks and shoots.

Setup: Fs at the blue line, D on the dot

Step 1: F passes to the D

Step 2: D behind the net; F loops low

Step 3: D passes to the F on the wall

Step 4: up the wall, curl at the cone

Step 5: attack the net and shoot

Back to your lines

F: pivot and keep your eyes on the passer
