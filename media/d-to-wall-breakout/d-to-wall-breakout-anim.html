Breakout: D to the Wall

F passes to the D and gets open on the wall. D skates it behind the net, hits the F, then steps up and defends 1-on-1.

Setup: Fs at the blue line, D on the dot

Step 1: F passes to the D

Step 2: D behind the net; F loops low

Step 3: D passes to the F on the wall

Step 4: Up the wall, curl. D steps up

Step 5: 1-on-1! D skates backwards

Back to your lines

D: gap up, then stay between the F and the net
