Half-ice 3v3

O tries to score. D must win the puck AND pass it to the coach at the blue line. Then O flips to D, fresh 3 on O.

Setup: 3 O, 3 D, coach at the blue line, fresh 3 waiting

Step 1: O carries in, passes to the slot

Step 2: O shoots

Step 3: D win the rebound, move it up to the coach

Change: D off, O flips to D, fresh 3 on O

Possession counts only once the coach gets the pass
