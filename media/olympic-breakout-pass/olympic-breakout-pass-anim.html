Olympic Breakout to 2-on-1

3-on-0 breakout to the blue line, tight turn, then back in 2-on-1 against the same D.

Setup: D at the net, lines in the neutral zone

Step 1: Coach rims it, D goes to get it

Step 2: D passes to W, C swings low

Step 3: W passes to C, D gaps up

Step 4: Carry it up to the blue line

Step 5: Tight turn at the blue line

Step 6: 2-on-1! C passes to W, W shoots

Back to your lines, next D to the net

Next group goes!
