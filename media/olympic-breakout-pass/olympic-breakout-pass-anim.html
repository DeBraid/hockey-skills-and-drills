Olympic Breakout Pass

Coach rims it in. D gets it and passes to the winger on the wall, who hits the centre curling low. Carry it out.

Setup: D in front of the net, lines at the blue line

Step 1: Coach rims it, D goes to get it

Step 2: D shoulder-checks, passes to W

Step 3: W passes to C in the middle

Step 4: C carries it out of the zone

Back to your lines, next D to the net

Next group goes!
