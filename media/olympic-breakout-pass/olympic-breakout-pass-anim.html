Olympic Breakout to 2-on-1

D gets the rim and hits the winger. Winger to the curling centre, out of the zone, then 2-on-1 against the D.

Setup: D at the net, lines at the blue line

Step 1: Coach rims it, D goes to get it

Step 2: D passes to W, C swings low

Step 3: W passes to C, D heads up ice

Step 4: Exit the zone, D races ahead

Step 5: 2-on-1, D skates backward

Step 6: C passes to W, W shoots!

Back to your lines, next D to the net
