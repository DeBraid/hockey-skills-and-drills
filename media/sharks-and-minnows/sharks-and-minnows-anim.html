Sharks and Minnows

Minnows with pucks try to cross the zone. Sharks knock pucks away. Lose your puck and you become a shark.

Setup: minnows on the blue line, sharks in the middle

Step 1: Go! Cross to the goal line

Step 2: Shark knocks the puck away

Caught? You're a shark now. Cross back!
