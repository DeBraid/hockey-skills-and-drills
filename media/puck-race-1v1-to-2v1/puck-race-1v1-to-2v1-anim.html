1v1 Puck Race to 2v1

Two players race for the puck. The winner attacks with a low-to-high pass; the loser defends the 2v1.

Setup: two racers side by side, coach with pucks

Step 1: coach rims it, race for the puck

Step 2: winner takes it up the wall

Step 3: low-to-high pass

Step 4: 2v1! Attack and shoot

Next pair races. Whoever gets the puck attacks.
