Rondo Passing

Keep-away in a small circle: 4-5 passers around the outside, 1-2 defenders in the middle.

Setup: groups of 5-7 in a small circle

Step 1: pass around, X pressures

Step 2: pass across, away from X

Step 3: next pass

Step 4: X intercepts!

Swap: X takes the passer's spot

Points for 5+ passes in a row. Rotate X every 30-60 s.
