2v2 Hard Rim

Rim it hard around the boards to your partner, then 2 on 2. D must touch the top of the circle first.

Setup: pucks in the lines at the hash marks, 2 D at the dots

Step 1: hard rim behind the net, D touch the top of the circle

Step 2: collect off the wall, 2v2 to the net

Step 3: shot!

D win it: get it to the next pair. D out, O to D, next pair rims
