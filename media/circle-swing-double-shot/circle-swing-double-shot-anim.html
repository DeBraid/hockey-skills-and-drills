Circle-Swing Pass and Drive

Twirl around the dot and pass to the red line. The receiver loops the centre cone and drives the net to score.

Setup: players by the goal-line cone with pucks, a receiver at the red line, a cone at centre

Step 1: Twirl around the dot with a puck

Step 2: Pass to the red line

Step 3: Receiver loops the centre cone

Step 4: Receiver drives the net and shoots!

Shooter becomes the receiver, receiver to the line

Next player goes when the receiver reaches the net. Passer becomes the receiver.
