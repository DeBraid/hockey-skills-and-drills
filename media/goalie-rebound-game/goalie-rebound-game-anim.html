Goalie Rebound Game

Two nets side by side. The coach shoots on either goalie; goalies try to steer rebounds into the other net.

Setup: two nets side by side, coach in the circle

Step 1: coach shoots on either goalie

Step 2: steer the rebound at the other net

Next shot, either net. Switch nets after 5-10 shots.

PassShotPucksGoaliesCoach (shooter)Each goalie starts on 10: -1 for a direct goal, -2 for a rebound steered in. First to 0 loses.
