Follow the Pass

Pass, follow your pass to that spot. Catch it at cone 3, drive and shoot.

Setup: a line in each corner, a player at every cone

Step 1: pass to cone 1 at the blue line, follow your pass

Step 2: pass to cone 2 on the boards, follow your pass

Step 3: pass to cone 3 at the far blue line, follow your pass

Step 4: catch it at cone 3, drive in and shoot!

Then join the back of the other line

Both sides go at the same time. Repeat!

Turn your phone sideways for a bigger rink.
