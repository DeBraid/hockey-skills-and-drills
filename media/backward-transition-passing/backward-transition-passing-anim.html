Backward Transition Passing

Skate forward, pivot to backward, and one-touch passes with the coach while skating backward around the circle.

Setup: both circles at once. Line at the dot, coach with pucks toward the blue line

Step 1: Skate forward to the wide cone

Step 2: Pivot, skate backward down the boards

Step 3: Coach passes, one-touch it back

Step 4: Backward around the circle and up the inside

Step 5: Second pass, one-touch it back

Step 6: Finish at the inside cone, then back to the line

Next player goes when the one ahead pivots. Then try Escapes!
