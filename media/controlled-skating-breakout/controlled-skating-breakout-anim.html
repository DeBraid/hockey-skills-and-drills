Controlled-Skating Breakout

D skates the puck behind the net while the F glides through 4 spots, showing a target at each. D passes on time; F attacks 1-on-0.

Setup: F at the blue line, D on the dot

Step 1: F passes to the D

Step 2: D behind the net; F to spot 1

Step 3: glide to spots 2 and 3

Step 4: spot 4; D passes on time

Step 5: carry out, curl at the cone

Step 6: attack 1-on-0 and shoot
