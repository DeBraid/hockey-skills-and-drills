Two-Line 2-on-0 Flow

Partners pass back and forth all the way up the ice, then attack 2-on-0 and shoot. Both ends go.

Setup: two lines below the goal line at each end

Step 1: C passes to W, both skate

Step 2: W passes back to C

Step 3: C passes to W again

Step 4: W passes to C in the middle

Step 5: C shoots, W drives the net

Join the lines at that end

Both ends go at the same time. Repeat!
