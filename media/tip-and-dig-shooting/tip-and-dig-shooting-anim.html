Tip-and-Dig Shooting

Tip the point shot into the empty net, dig it out, and shoot on the goalie.

Setup: empty net at the hash marks, tipper in front

Step 1: point shot, T tips it into the empty net

Step 2: T digs it out, shooter moves in to tip

Step 3: shoot on the goalie!

Back of the line; the shooter is the new tipper

Run the same setup in the other corner too
