Low-to-High: Give-and-Go

Carry in, drive to the dot, curl, pass up to the D. Give-and-go.

Setup: F line at the red line, D at the points

Step 1: carry in, drive down to the dot

Step 2: curl back toward the boards

Step 3: pass up to the D (low-to-high)

Step 4: cut to the slot, D passes back

Step 5: F shoots!

Then F joins the back of the line

Repeat with the next forward
