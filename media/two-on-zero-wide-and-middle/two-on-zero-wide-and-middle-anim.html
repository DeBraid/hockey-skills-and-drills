2-on-0 Wide and Middle

One goes wide with the puck, one drives the middle to the back post. Shoot (or pass), and both go to the net.

Setup: wide line at the boards, middle line

Step 1: go wide; partner drives the middle

Step 2: cut in; middle goes to the back post

Step 3: shoot! Both go to the net

Switch lines

Or pass across to the back post. Switch lanes.
