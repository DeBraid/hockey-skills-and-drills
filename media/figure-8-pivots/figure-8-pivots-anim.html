Figure-8s with Pivots

Skate a figure-8 around the two circles: forwards around the top circle, pivot to backwards at the cone on the bottom circle, pivot back to forwards at the next cone.

Setup: line between the circles

Step 1: top circle, skating forwards

Step 2: pivot to backwards at cone 1

Step 3: pivot forwards at cone 2, finish

Back of the line

Next skater goes when you reach cone 1
