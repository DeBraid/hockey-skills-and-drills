Zone Entries: Delay

Carry in wide, delay on the wall, then attack (top) or hit a catcher streaking to the slot (bottom).

Setup: top = Delay, bottom = Delay, low-to-slot

Step 1: Delay: carry in wide

Step 2: stop and curl back up the wall

Step 3: attack the net, shoot

Step 4: low-to-slot: carry in wide

Step 5: delay; the catcher goes

Step 6: catcher streaks to the net, pass

Step 7: catch and shoot

Back to your lines

Other variations: see Low-to-High Give-and-Go and D-to-D
