Mirror Angling 2v1

F loops the cones while the D mirrors around the dot, then a quick 2v1 rush. Both attackers drive the net.

Setup: F line by the cones, D (X) line at the circle

Step 1: F loops the cone, X mirrors

Step 2: round cone 2, X angles, F2 drives

Step 3: pass to F2 at the net

Step 4: F2 shoots

Back to your lines, next group

Puck carrier can pass, shoot or delay on the wall
