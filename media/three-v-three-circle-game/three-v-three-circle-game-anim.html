3v3 Circle Game

3 on 3 at a faceoff circle with two nets. Only one player per team inside the circle.

Setup: 2 nets in the circle, 1 inside and 2 outside per team

Step 1: coach puts a puck in to an outside X

Step 2: move it around the outside

Step 3: pass into the inside X, quick shot

Outside players can shoot too. Rotate inside and outside
