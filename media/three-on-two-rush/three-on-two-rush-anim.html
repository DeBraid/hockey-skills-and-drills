3-on-2 Rush

Three forwards attack two D. Puck wide, middle drives the net, far wing goes back post.

Setup: 3 lines at centre, 2 D at the blue line

Step 1: Go! D skate backwards

Step 2: Puck to the wing

Step 3: Middle drives the net, far wing back post

Step 4: Shoot for a rebound!

Forwards back to centre, switch lines

Next 3 go. D: one on the puck, one on the net
