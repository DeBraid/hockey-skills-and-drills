Triangle Passing on the Move

Groups of 3 skate up the ice in a triangle: middle to wing, across to the far wing, back to the middle, shoot.

Setup: 3 lines at centre, pucks in the middle

Step 1: Go! Middle passes to a wing

Step 2: Pass across to the far wing

Step 3: Pass to the middle, drive the net

Step 4: Shoot!

Switch lines and skate back to centre

Next group! Keep the triangle, sticks on the ice
