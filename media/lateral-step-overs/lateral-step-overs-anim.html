Lateral Step-overs with Pivots

Sideways crossover step-overs down a lane of cones, pivoting 180 degrees at each cone.

Setup: 3 lanes of cones, benches at the top

Step 1: step over sideways to cone 1, facing the benches

Step 2: pivot, step over to cone 2, back to the benches

Step 3: pivot, step over to cone 3, facing the benches

Back up the outside to your line

Stay low, chest up, stick on the ice

SkateConeSkatersPivot at each cone: face the benches, then away from them, then face them again.
