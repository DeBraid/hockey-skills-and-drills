2v2 Point Shot Small Area Game

2 on 2 in a corner with a point player. Dump in, battle, use the point for shots, regroup on a turnover.

Setup: one group of 5 per corner net (2 O, 2 D, point)

Step 1: point dumps it in, 2v2 battle starts

Step 2: O wins it, works it up to the point

Step 3: point shot, O at the net for tips

Turnover: regroup through the point. Goal or 60 s: swap
