D-to-Wall Breakout to 2-on-1

Same breakout: D skates it behind the net and hits the winger on the wall. The centre swings with him. 2-on-1 back in against the D.

Setup: wingers + centres at the blue line, D on the dot

Step 1: F passes to the D

Step 2: D behind the net; winger loops low

Step 3: D passes to the winger on the wall

Step 4: Both curl at the cones. D steps up

Step 5: 2-on-1! Pass across. D backs up

Step 6: Centre shoots!
